# What a relation connects

An extraction result should let two readers answer different questions about the same prediction. A reader highlighting evidence wants the exact words. A reader indexing document entities wants to know which people or organizations those words referred to in a particular resolution run. The existing `Signal`, `Track`, and `Identity` types give these answers different scopes. The following examples explain when to project between them and when to retain the original observation. [Grounded extraction decisions](grounded-extraction.md) · [Credit corrections](credit-corrections.md)

## Keep the two endpoints and their evidence

Consider “Maya founded Acme. She later sold it.” A mention-level prediction can identify “She” and “it” as arguments of *sold*. Resolving those mentions can produce a second, document-level answer about Maya and Acme. If a reviewer reassigns “She” to a different track, the source locations and the model’s original argument selection still stand. A writer materializing the projected answer should record the document edition or recompute against the updated document. A consumer receiving only track IDs cannot recover which sentence supplied the evidence.

A CPU run of the trained GLiNER-Relex export on that exact two-sentence input makes the distinction concrete. With entity labels `person,organization` and relation labels `founded,sold`, it emitted three mentions: “Maya” at 0..4, “Acme” at 13..17, and “She” at 19..22. Its edges were `founded(Maya, Acme)` (0.9968) and `sold(She, Acme)` (0.8639). The second edge crosses sentence boundaries and there is no predicted mention for “it”. A consumer could use the edge to retrieve both sentences. It could not reconstruct an `it → Acme` coreference prediction from that output. These scores come from one diagnostic input. [Relex architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10108)

The flat `Relation` already carries a directed head and tail, a predicate, an optional trigger range, and a score. Preserve that API for extractors. At the grounded boundary, resolve an endpoint to an individual signal only when the source geometry, label, and surface identify it uniquely. Preserve disjoint segments. A midpoint or bounding interval can identify text the model never selected. A signal can participate before anyone assigns it a track. A checked view can obtain the current memberships without allocating a global identifier for every prediction. Extractor provenance and the source of a claim in a quoted sentence answer different questions. [Flat types](../../crates/anno/src/core/entity.rs) · [Grounded types](../../crates/anno/src/core/grounded/mod.rs) · [Source offsets](../CONTRACT.md#source-text-and-offsets)

Grounded documents now persist relation evidence against the document ID and source digest. Reading a mention edge after a track edit preserves its original signal endpoints and projects their current memberships. The reverse projection needs equal care. A DWIE gold relation joins two concepts. Its endpoints may each have several mentions spread over a document. Expanding those endpoints into a Cartesian product invents mention-pair evidence and lets frequently mentioned entities dominate the metric. A native concept edge can be evaluated as an entity-level prediction even when the annotator supplied no specific trigger. Report supplied-cluster and predicted-cluster results separately. An exact-membership score also penalizes an otherwise correct endpoint for one extra pronoun. Pair it with a graded coreference or official entity-centric score when that distinction matters to the reader. [DWIE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12626) · [Reference evaluation](https://github.com/klimzaporojets/DWIE)

There is a stronger counterexample to identifying endpoints by span equality. In the Re-DocRED development document *Cambodia–Vietnam Friendship Monument*, two separately identified gold entities share the same annotated span set and type. Across the 500 development documents, 18 of 17,284 directed gold triples involve ambiguous endpoints under exact span-set matching. Retaining supplied entity IDs makes all 18 scoreable. For predicted IDs, even identical files cannot establish that correspondence from spans alone. Count the ambiguous edges and give a score interval. Using the gold relation label to break the tie leaks the answer being evaluated. These are measurements on the published development file, SHA-256 `051ee1d057204a5d08ef5502beacdadf191245b5eaf0e29ec2c607cf002c016f`. [Development annotations](https://github.com/tonytan48/Re-DocRED/blob/main/data/dev_revised.json) · [Exact-endpoint scorer](../../crates/anno-eval/examples/docred/score.py)

DWIE supplies the opposite counterexample. In document `DW_1095032`, 14 of 15 concepts have text mentions. One of five relation triples involves a concept with zero mentions (the original edge is `(7, gpe0, 14)`). On an exact source replay the anchored cohort recovers four edges. With the supplied concept IDs all five are scoreable. An evaluator that requires every conceptual endpoint to own a span must either invent supporting words or lose that fifth edge. The DWIE adapter reports those edges separately, verifies offsets against the original article, and accepts supplied IDs for scoring the complete concept inventory. This is a corpus annotation and supplies no independent test of model accuracy. [Original annotation](https://github.com/klimzaporojets/DWIE/blob/master/data/annos/DW_1095032.json) · [DWIE scorer adapter](../../crates/anno-eval/examples/docred/score_dwie.py)

## Identity has narrower semantics than anaphora

In “Maya met Ada. They left,” the plural expression refers to a group. Unioning its two antecedents would merge two distinct people. In “I bought a bike. The wheel squeaks,” the wheel is associated with the bike. In “Maya resigned. That surprised me,” the antecedent can be a proposition. Each case has a legitimate target and each requires a different interpretation. Preserve the existing invariant that a committed ordinary track expresses document-local co-membership. A model supporting group reference should emit a group with member references or a typed anaphoric link, with evaluation that exercises those outputs. A binary identity-only resolver can report its narrower coverage. [Universal Anaphora scorer](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.521/)

Anno already represents discontinuous locations, event arguments and event relations. Its FRECO predicate `is_coreferent()` includes causal and temporal links for that task, while `is_strict()` tests full identity. Using the former to build components of identical events would merge a cause with its effect. Keep the annotation scheme attached at conversion and test the operation a downstream consumer intends. Multilingual systems can also predict references without an overt surface mention. Fabricating a zero-length highlight would violate the text-anchored signal contract. CorPipe 25 is a concrete case. Its released multilingual models operate on CoNLL-U token identifiers, including empty nodes. Preserve the source sentence and token ID for an implicit argument and offer a character range only when one exists. A text-highlighting client can then distinguish an unavailable location from an empty name. [Anno event relation definitions](../../crates/anno/src/core/types/framing.rs) · [CorPipe 25 input and output](https://github.com/ufal/crac2025-corpipe) · [Multilingual shared task](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21369)

## A claim can be extracted accurately and still be denied

“Jo denied selling Acme to Maya for €2m” supplies a potential seller, buyer, thing sold, price, and a source disputing the sale. Four independent binary facts would lose which sale connects those arguments. Marking the Acme signal as negated would put denial on the wrong object. An event-aware producer should reuse Anno’s role-bearing event representation where it fits. A consumer publishing assertions needs to retain who expresses what attitude toward which event, and where that interpretation came from. An extraction confidence measures success at reading the sentence. It supplies no probability that the sale happened. [Anno framing types](../../crates/anno/src/core/types/framing.rs) · [FactBank](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2009T23) · [DyGIE++ event and relation extraction](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1585/)

A downstream publisher may independently retract an attribution while a second source continues to support it. Anno’s catalog examples already exercise this behavior. Keep edition, issuer, visibility, and withdrawal at the publication boundary. An inference model cannot supply those decisions. A later source correction can invalidate a published edge even while its historic extraction remains an accurate reading of the earlier text. [Publication and corrections](credit-corrections.md)

The same separation is measurable in a trained document extractor. On the JEREX authors’ first joint test document, *Jeff Prosserman*, the checkpoint recovered all 31 mention clusters. Adding the entity-type requirement reduced that to 30. The model predicted 14 directed relations. Seven matched the nine annotated edges. Passing the source and entity checks therefore says something specific and limited about the later relation decision. In Anno’s namesake fixture, Maverick also produced different cluster members when given the marked mentions instead of proposing its own spans. Keep the mention-supply policy with each evaluation. [JEREX replay and evaluator](../QA.md#run-the-jerex-joint-checkpoint) · [Namesake replay](track-association.md)

## A graph constraint has a scope

If a schema requires each person to have at most one employer, each of two windows can return a feasible graph and their union can still give the same person two employers. GLiNER2's upstream long-text implementation merges locally selected edges. Establishing document-wide feasibility needs either one complete document decode or a second assignment over the combined candidates. `anno joint` maps each window’s candidates into document coordinates, keeps the strongest score for duplicate candidates, and solves the combined graph once. A pair whose endpoints never occur together in a scoring window has no model score. Global feasibility therefore describes the selected graph over the observed candidates. Keep the schema, input revision, endpoint ID scope and decoder-reported feasibility with its result. A schema-derived inverse edge and a separately scored reverse edge can have identical endpoints but support different explanations. [Upstream window merge](https://github.com/fastino-ai/GLiNER2/blob/5be80525b648a2fb20246958421b885ae9e49fbb/gliner2/joint_ie/long_text.py) · [Anno worker](../../crates/anno-cli/src/cli/commands/joint.py)

## Model scores depend on the choice being made

Anno already measured upstream GLiNER2 and GLiNER2.5 on 119 held-out Dutch CoNLL-2002 articles. At their development-selected thresholds the older checkpoint found 203 correct mentions with 62 extra suggestions. GLiNER2.5 found 204 with 201 extra. A reviewer would see 139 extra false suggestions for one extra correct one on this panel. A threshold, candidate population, and model revision belong with the run. A field named `confidence` on an emitted entity cannot by itself reproduce that decision. Report both the score scale and the target decision when a caller needs calibration. [Anno comparison and intervals](https://github.com/arclabs561/anno/pull/219)

## Evidence needed before widening the public schema

| Proposed capability | Fixture and consumer operation |
|---|---|
| Constrained entity–relation graph | Compare a typed, directed edge with upstream decoding on the same Unicode source. Include two entities of the same type, swapped endpoints, and competing labels. Boundary-head tensor parity alone leaves graph selection untested. [GLiNER2.5](https://huggingface.co/fastino/gliner2.5-multi-v1) |
| Document-level relation with evidence | Change one track assignment while keeping the mention-level prediction fixed. The old highlight must remain reproducible and the projected answer must change. Compare with a document-level system that jointly models coreference and relation links. [JEREX](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.319/) · [2026 joint model](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44443-026-00624-7) |
| Group reference or bridging | Ask separately for distinct people, group members, and parts. Verify that the individual identity index still contains the right number of people. [Universal Anaphora](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.521/) |
| A calibrated score | Name the scored outcome, candidate set, policy, and population. Measure separately whether both mentions exist, whether a pair links, and whether an entire track is correct. [Anno association decisions](track-association.md) |
| Missing edge in gold | Record whether the task uses a closed label set and whether that pair was reviewed. A benchmark can score a missing label as negative while a publication system keeps it unknown. Re-DocRED added missing triples across 4,053 reannotated documents. [Re-DocRED](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.580/) |

Two published directions remain comparisons for the design. Grenander and colleagues compress entity history in incremental seq2seq coreference, and Du and colleagues jointly update document-level coreference and relation graphs. For this review we could verify the papers, but found no released checkpoint and inference contract for either that we could run in Anno. Their reported results do not establish compatibility with Anno’s T5 decoder or JEREX runner. [Incremental seq2seq paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14504) · [Document-level joint paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44443-026-00624-7)

These distinctions can be adopted independently. A backend that emits prompt-labeled text spans needs no event, coreference, or publication fields. The point for adding a type is a producer with a distinct observation and a reader whose answer would otherwise change or become unrecoverable.

# Evidence for three outstanding model families

Reviewed 25 November 2026. The evidence that would make a text-to-prediction replay possible differs by family.

| Model | Public material inspected | Executable path in Anno | Remaining dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original TPLinker / TPLinkerPlus | The original authors provide preprocessing, training, decoding and a link for model states. An author subsequently says the saved checkpoint was lost. The repository metadata lists no recognized license. | For TPLinker, the exporter accepts a caller-provided compatible trained three-head state and its dataset's relation vocabulary. The Plus example accepts sparse token pairs, entity and relation vocabularies, and Unicode token offsets from an upstream inference run. | For original Plus inference from text, a trained **single-head** checkpoint, matching handshaking/tag layout, tokenizer, window policy, and redistribution terms. A base encoder or an original three-head TPLinker export cannot supply the Plus predictions. [Original code](https://github.com/131250208/TPlinker-joint-extraction) · [Checkpoint author statement](https://github.com/131250208/TPlinker-joint-extraction/issues/89#issuecomment-3695039976) · [Anno decoder](../../crates/anno/examples/decode_tplinker_plus.rs) |
| Grenander, Cohen and Steedman, 2025 | The paper describes a T0-3B generator, compression of entity histories after each chunk, special tokens, and experiments on OntoNotes and LitBank. The arXiv article carries CC BY 4.0. The author's public sentence-incremental code describes their 2022 EMNLP system. Their incremental-coref fork describes Xia and Van Durme's earlier models. | Anno's T5 neural resolver can run its own compatible graphs. The 2025 trained state, generation vocabulary, compressed-history update implementation and checkpoint license were unverified. | An author-matched checkpoint and decoder or an independently trained implementation with its own evidence. The published LitBank and OntoNotes scores belong to the paper's trained systems. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14504) · [2022 system](https://github.com/mgrenander/sentence-incremental-coref) · [Earlier reader](https://github.com/mgrenander/incremental-coref) |
| Du et al., document-level multi-task explicit interaction, 2026 | The paper gives the architecture and reports experiments. Its data availability statement directs readers to the corresponding author on reasonable request. The article carries CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. | Anno has a JEREX runner for its own trained architecture. This paper's trained checkpoint, code/weight terms, exact preprocessing, graph updates and inference entry point were unverified. | Release of compatible trained parameters and inference code, or a separately validated reimplementation. The article license applies to the article. [Paper and data availability](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44443-026-00624-7) |

**Community TPLinker candidates have different coverage.** JunnYu's PyTorch DuIE project reports trained TPLinkerPlus relation results and describes differences from the original architecture. Its README documents training from a base encoder. The Chinese NER implementation by taishan1994 links a model/data bundle and demonstrates **entities only**. Luozhouyang's Apache-2.0 reimplementation links processed NYT data. These projects establish additional training paths, but this review did not verify a retrievable, licensed, joint Plus checkpoint with tokenizer, label IDs and matching reference predictions. A public text-to-entity example from another head cannot validate directed relations. [DuIE training and results](https://github.com/JunnYu/GPLinker_pytorch) · [Chinese NER-only model/data](https://github.com/taishan1994/pytorch_TPLinker_Plus_Ner) · [TPLinker NYT reimplementation](https://github.com/luozhouyang/TPLinker)

The search covered the linked papers, author repositories, the original issue tracker, Hugging Face model names containing `tplinker`, `tp-linker`, `mgrenander` or `seq2seq-coref`, and community implementation READMEs. A similarly named checkpoint, missing search result, or paper license alone cannot identify a weight file and its inference contract. These are bounded search results as of the review date.

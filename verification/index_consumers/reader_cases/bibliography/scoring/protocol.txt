Dated bibliography scoring protocol, frozen before seeing holdout answers or predictions.

Frame: all 17 consecutive remaining 2023 eLife notices from the previously frozen 29-row registration frame (publisher/sample.json eligible[12:]). Join by notice DOI and keep its target work DOI and notice date. No downsampling, replacement, or removal of hard cases. Report both notices and distinct works. A query is one notice and one of three cuts, for 51 queries. The three cuts on a work are dependent. Delivery modes are paired repeats, not extra observations. No population rates or significance claims.

Cut first: earliest publisher-released edition available in an index covering the start of the work. Cut pre: latest edition with publisher UTC release day strictly before the notice day. Cut post: latest edition with release day through the end of the notice UTC day. An edition on the notice day need not exist. If day-only precision cannot resolve the applicable edition, gold records the ambiguity component-wise. Never substitute original publication date for a revision's release date. Work DOI identifies the target, notice DOI identifies this sampled correction. A publisher release date measures the edition's effective date; the capture watermark bounds what the replay inspected. This experiment does not reconstruct what a subscriber actually saw in real time.

Two arms receive the same captured work records. Dated applies the requested cut. Mutable substitutes the actual latest release at capture, even if it postdates the notice. Missing current evidence yields abstention in mutable just as missing selected historical evidence does in dated. Keep the same source metadata and abstention reason regardless of delivery. Evaluate per-request reads and a bulk read for the same evidence watermark. Require equality of the returned edition, byline, evidence locator/digest/license/date, availability and abstention reason. Duplicate and out-of-order requests are separate functional checks. The comparison of the two call modes alone cannot establish streaming ingestion, cache invalidation latency or subscriber convergence.

Independent curator supplies (notice, cut, status, edition, byline, reason, sources). Gold status verified supports both edition and ordered byline. Partial supports exactly the supplied component(s). Null components remain unjudged; unavailable or ambiguous may retain a separately verified component, with rationale. Every non-null component is a claim the curator considers scorable. The canonical byline is an ordered list of ordered [field,value] pairs for each named person or collaboration, including prefix/suffix and duplicates. Its comparison key is the literal author count and SHA-256 of UTF-8 compact JSON (ensure_ascii=False). Surface values follow the frozen publisher component normalization. Original XML author count alone never establishes equality. Published credit does not assert personal identity.

Count selected, answered and abstained for every arm/cut. For each reference component count reference-known, emitted-and-scorable, exact, wrong, unresolved answers and abstained-on-known. Byline wrong/scorable and answered/all use different denominators. Report joint exact when both edition and byline have reference support. Count works with any verified error for each component. Show paired byline gains and losses, retaining abstentions and separating wrong answers from recovery. If precision divides by zero use undefined or show raw counts. Complete byline agreement when pre/post gold differs measures whether the output captured the change; report the denominator. Count semantic delivery mismatches after the same supplied snapshot (zero is the invariant).

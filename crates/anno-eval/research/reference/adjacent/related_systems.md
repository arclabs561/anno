# Where a joint resolver can still help

Research checked 25 November 2026. The published evaluations below use different corpora, inputs and metrics. These are design constraints for the experiments here, rather than a common leaderboard.

| Decision | Evidence from related systems | Opportunity and comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Recover a speaker and the people addressed | Joint quotation attribution and coreference already has a structured prediction precedent. PDNC also labels internal character mentions and reporting expressions. A November 2026 preprint reports mean BookNLP direct-speech detection F1 of .88 on LitBank and .77 on its NightmareDiscourseBench under its approximate string matcher. A perfect quote boundary silently removes one failure mode. | Compare independently predicted roles, a simple joint role scorer and a learned disagreement resolver. Repeat with predicted quote boundaries, unmatched predicted quotes, and author-disjoint evaluation. Track both roles right, each role alone and false attributions. [Joint quotation model](https://aclanthology.org/E14-1005/) · [PDNC pipeline](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-short.64/) · [Boundary stress test, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27059) |
| Find the antecedent and decide its relation | LLMBridge distinguishes raw-text resolution, supplied gold anaphors and supplied mentions. DiscoTrack evaluates detection, subtype and antecedent as separate prompts, with different task/language coverage. A subtype answer on a supplied pair can succeed even when a caller cannot find the pair. | Evaluate exact source pair plus edge type at a fixed review budget. Isolate span misses, candidate misses and ranking misses. Give identity, bridging, group membership and discourse-segment reference their own output types. Preserve multiple subtype labels. [LLMBridge](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1073/) · [DiscoTrack](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1020/) |
| Use a catalog to inform identity | DuCEI selectively runs metadata refinement on 27.8% of mentions at about 1.27 times its stage-one cost. Its pruning recall is conditional on the gold entity already being present. Prior-based systems have difficulty with rare names and missing entries. | Report new-to-document, in-cache, in-retrieval-set and NIL separately. Measure wrong merges when a predicted page is reused. Trigger additional retrieval on ambiguity and verify whether the resulting links justify its cost. [DuCEI](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1298/) · [ZELDA](https://github.com/flairNLP/zelda) |
| Reconcile entity types, aliases and relations | DWIE evaluates entities across a document. SciIE shares spans across three scientific extraction tasks. A 2026 DocRE preprint rescored identical predictions on DocRED and Re-DocRED and found a reversal in the ranking of consistency-repair methods. Some apparently missing inverses arise from annotation incompleteness. | Compare joint and pipeline decoders with identical spans and retrieval sets. Report relation gains together with extra false identity pairs. Let Heyting return unknown for unsupported facts. Audit a sample of newly inferred edges before treating annotation absence as error. [DWIE](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2021.102563) · [SciIE](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1360/) · [Annotation closure preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24312) |
| Predict semantic types and ancestors | Box typing models already exploit containment. DR-FET uses label descriptors and retrieval to reduce a large label space and filter noisy distant supervision. Both label retrieval and supervision quality can dominate the choice of scoring geometry. | Compare Subsume with a Tuplet point scorer using the same encoder, retrieved types, hard negatives and training budget. Measure rare-label recall, subtype consistency and calibration separately. [Box typing](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.160/) · [DR-FET](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1584/) |
| Track an event and its participants across sources | uCDCR finds ECB+ has relatively low lexical diversity across its harmonized corpora. A reannotation study reports a same-head-lemma baseline of 69.91 CoNLL F1 on its ECB+ comparison and 52.92 on ECB+r for non-singleton chains. | Score same-lemma, different-lemma and different-event same-participant cases. Use all test documents or document retrieval, report event candidate recall and recheck on a second corpus. Adding a common participant can link two separate meetings. [uCDCR](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.328/) · [Lexically rich reference, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17424) |

**The closest competitor to bounded entity memory already exists.** PortNLP's CRAC 2026 submission uses a rolling context and a registry of up to 30 active entities, scored by frequency and recency. The shared task reports 77.11 primary CoNLL F1 for the winning unconstrained system and 74.32 for the winning LLM-track system on CorefUD 1.4. CorPipe also describes predicting empty nodes, mentions and coreference jointly. For a new resolver, offering a bounded registry or shared decoder alone would duplicate an existing design. The open comparison is which *returning, hard-to-rank references* are recovered at matched memory and review budgets, with predicted histories and explicit accounting for zero mentions. Our lexical and gold-history diagnostics use different inputs and metrics from this leaderboard. [Shared-task findings](https://aclanthology.org/2026.codi-1.21/) · [PortNLP](https://aclanthology.org/2026.codi-1.25/) · [CorPipe](https://aclanthology.org/2026.codi-1.27/)

The opportunity for Anno is a source-backed correction a reviewer can accept at the right granularity. A quote can have the right speaker and the wrong addressee. Two event reports can share a participant and describe different events. A known catalog identity can help retrieve evidence while still yielding a bad document cluster. For Sheaf, the interesting evaluation subset is disagreement between independently observable views. For Ricci, it is whether graph context improves the same held-out decisions over a text-only model without passing gold identities into the graph.

NILK 1.0 limited catalog replay, frozen before opening the training or test windows.

Use training shard 01 and the official test file. From each take four 2 MiB byte windows starting at 0, floor(size/4), floor(size/2), and floor(3*size/4). Discard the potentially partial line at each nonzero start and the trailing incomplete line. Score all remaining test records from pages absent from the training windows. Report page overlap, duplicate observation IDs and both splits' sampled sizes. The selection is deterministic and byte-weighted. Its windows are not a random or representative population of mentions.

Construct a small earlier-catalog lookup from the printed mentions and labeled IDs of non-NIL training examples only. Test identity, NIL label, context and hyperlink target are withheld from lookup. Compare codepoint-exact spelling and casefolding with canonical decomposition and combining-mark removal. Multiple distinct candidate IDs or no candidate means abstain. For known queries distinguish a correct unique link, wrong unique link, abstention with a reachable ID, and absence of the ID from our sampled lookup. For old-catalog NIL, count every released link as wrong. Separately count cases with a newer retrospective QID. A held-out name absent from this lookup is insufficient evidence of old-catalog NIL.

Conditioned on the NIL label being given, compare same-spelling pair suggestions across different test pages with equality of the retrospective QIDs. Exclude same-page pairs. Report opportunities, correctly joined, incorrectly joined and missed pairs. This is a clustering diagnostic separate from NIL detection. No thresholds, tuning, external aliases or production promotions.
